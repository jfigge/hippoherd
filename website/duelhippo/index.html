Obstacle course

Thirty rings over the countryside, climbing, diving and turning. Fly through them in order: the clock runs from the first to the last, and you have twice a perfect run's time to finish. Each level packs the rings closer and the turns tighter, and scores more a ring. Finish a level to open the next.
